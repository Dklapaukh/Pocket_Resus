Morphology review: VF is irregular without organised QRS complexes; VT is regular with broad complexes;
torsades demonstrates changing polarity and waxing/waning amplitude. The supplied labels and these
features were checked during implementation; this is not independent clinical certification.

Clinical references
https://cpg.stjohn.org.nz/tabs/guidelines/cardiac-eas/page/cardiac-arrest-eas
EAS 3.14, version 1.1.0.1 dated 16 June 2026, checked live 28 October 2026.
Torsades history requires withholding amiodarone even after a later rhythm change.
Magnesium: adults 10 mmol IV over 1-2 minutes; repeat once if torsades persists/recurs.
PEA: address reversible causes; 0.9% NaCl IV 1-2 L adults, 20-40 mL/kg children.

https://cpg.stjohn.org.nz/tabs/medicines/page/magnesium-sulphate-eas
EAS 14.27, version 1.0.5.3 dated 9 June 2025, checked live 28 October 2026.
Adult cardiac-arrest magnesium is diluted to 10 mL for 1-2 minute administration.
Avoid co-administration in an IV line with an adrenaline infusion; document in mmol.

Packaged cpg-paediatric-equipment.pdf, EAS 7.2, exported 24 September 2026:
Magnesium doses for bands 20 / 30 / 40 kg: 4 / 6 / 8 mmol.
5 / 10 kg tables contain no magnesium dose, so the app gives no numeric reference for these bands.
Paediatric preparation #13: dilute 5 mL from 10 mmol/5 mL ampoule to 10 mL (1 mmol/mL).
